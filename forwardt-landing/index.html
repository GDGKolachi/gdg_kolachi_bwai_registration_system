01 / The gap

You ship in days. Verification takes weeks.

AI writes more of your code than ever — and it ships with more bugs, not fewer. The bottleneck moved from writing software to verifying it. For consumer apps you can crowd-test. For B2B, fintech, and internal tools, you can't hand login access to a stranger in another country — so QA becomes the thing that's blocking your release.

02 / What we do

Supervised QA. Not crowdtesting.

Crowd platforms hand your build to whoever's online. That works until your software needs real access — to a staging environment, to sensitive data, to anything under NDA. We don't scatter access across a crowd. We broker it: testers work inside a controlled session or on a secured device, access lives with us, never with an individual, and every result clears a review before it reaches you.
