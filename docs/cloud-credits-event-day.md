# Google Cloud credits: event-day guide

For organizers and volunteers. One page, no code.

## 1. What changes

When you scan an attendee's QR code at check-in, they now get an email with a short walkthrough video, the link to claim their Google Cloud credits, and an **I've redeemed my credits** button. When they press it, we see it live on the **Cloud Credits** page, so we know who has finished and who needs help.

## 2. Before the event (organizers)

- **Events** -> edit the event -> **Google Cloud credits** section:
  - Switch on **Send credits email on QR check-in**.
  - **Claim link**: the universal claim link. For a new event it is prefilled; replace it if the event has its own.
  - **Walkthrough video link (optional)**: an unlisted YouTube link. Also prefilled on new events.
- Rehearse with real inboxes (Gmail, Outlook, a university address) and a phone: how fast does it arrive, does it land in spam, does the video play on mobile data?
- On the **Cloud Credits** page, use **For the projector**: put the link or QR on a slide so people can get the email again themselves.

## 3. At the check-in desk (volunteers)

**QR Scan** (or **Mobile Check-in**)
1. Scan the attendee's QR code.
2. Press **Mark as attended** (if they never confirmed their spot, you will be asked first: press **Allow anyway**).
3. Watch the credits line under "Already checked in": **queued**, then **sent**. If it says **failed**, press **Retry**.
4. Scanning someone twice is safe. It never sends a second email, and it now shows their credits status.

**Checked in by name** (**Check-in** page): press **Send credits email** on their row. Desk check-in on its own does not send the email.

**Hackathon**: the scanner sends the email. The manual list button does not, so use **Send credits email** for those people.

## 4. During the event (Cloud Credits page)

- Live counts: checked in, emails sent, failed, redeemed.
- **Follow up** list: people checked in who have not confirmed, oldest first. Rows turn highlighted after 15 minutes. Walk over and help them.
- **Email failed** tab: press **Resend** next to anyone listed.
- Organizers only: **Email everyone checked in who hasn't got it** sends to all who are missing it.

## 5. Fallbacks in the room

- **Show credits QR**: open the person's record, press it, and the attendee scans it with their phone to reach the confirm page.
- **Mark redeemed**: only after you have seen the **GDP Credit** billing account on their screen.
- **Undo** a mistaken confirmation (organizers only).
- The "Didn't get the email?" page on the projector lets attendees request the email again.

## 6. Tips for attendees

- Use a personal @gmail.com account.
- Open the claim link in an Incognito window.
- Keep your phone ready: you will scan a "verify you're human" QR code.
- Credits take 3-5 minutes to show up.

## 7. If something goes wrong

| Symptom | What to do |
|---|---|
| Email not arriving | Ask them to check spam. Then press **Resend** (Cloud Credits page or their record), or use **Show credits QR**. |
| Status says failed | Press **Resend** and read the error shown next to it. |
| Nothing happens on scan | Refresh the page. Or find them on the **Check-in** page and press **Send credits email**. |
| Projector page says it resent, but nothing arrives | They are not checked in yet, or they already asked within the last 2 minutes. |
